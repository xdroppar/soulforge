What it does

Everything in one place

Built by a Deadlock player for Deadlock players. Every number shows how many games it comes from, so you know what to trust.

App

A coach that's watched your games

After every game it finds the habit that's costing you the most, and shows you the clips where it happened.

  • Why you died, sorted by how often
  • Your item timings against top players on your hero
  • One thing to work on, not twenty
App

See what the next rank does differently

Your Soul Score rates each part of your game against players at your rank. Your journey shows how far you've come and what the ranks above you do better.

App

A score for every part of your game

Farming, laning, objectives, survival, aim and fighting, each out of 100 against players at your rank.

App

Your lobby before the match

Everyone's rank and main the second you load in, your lane matchup and your odds. While you play: win chance, timers, and a nudge when you're sitting on too many souls.

  • No overlay, nothing injected into the game
App

Every kill and death, already clipped

If you record with OBS, every kill and death is cut into its own clip when the game ends. Find a play in seconds, or make a reel.

Questions

FAQ

When is it out?

Soon. It's being tested right now. Sign up above and you'll get it first.

Is it free?

Yes.

Can it get me banned?

It doesn't touch the game. No overlay, nothing injected or loaded into Deadlock. It reads public match data and the log file the game writes itself.

Will it lower my FPS?

While Deadlock is running it only reads a text file and makes a few web requests. Heavy work, like cutting clips, waits until the game closes.

Mac or Linux?

Windows only for now. The website version will work anywhere.
