Getting started

  1. Run SoulforgeSetup.exe

    Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the beta isn't signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. No admin needed.

  2. Pick your Steam account

    SOULFORGE lists the Steam accounts signed in on your PC. Click yours, and it reads your recent games (about half a minute, once).

  3. Turn on Live

    In Steam, right-click Deadlock, then Properties, then General. Add -condebug to Launch options. SOULFORGE shows you how, and goes green once it sees your game.

While we test it

It updates itself

New versions download in the background, never while you're in a game. Click Restart in the top bar, or it installs next time you close it.

Found something broken?

Settings, About, Report a problem. You see everything in the report before you copy it, then send it to whoever gave you the link.

Doesn't touch the game

No overlay, no injection, nothing loaded into Deadlock. It reads public match data and the game's own log file.

SOULFORGE is a fan-made tool. Not made by or affiliated with Valve. Match data from deadlock-api.com.
